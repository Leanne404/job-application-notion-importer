Automatic Website fallback

Open your private Apps Script tool, then click this extension icon to pair that tab. After setup, use its normal Preview websites and Fill blank fields buttons. No copying or pasting is needed.

Enable on this tool tab

Chrome will ask for access to HTTP and HTTPS websites so the extension can open failed job links and read their rendered pages automatically. Only requests from the paired tool tab are accepted. Job tabs are closed afterward. Pair again if you close the tool tab or restart Chrome.

Local AI uses Qwen2.5 0.5B on your device. The first failed job may download hundreds of MB from Hugging Face. No AI account, API key or cloud credits are used. Keep the local AI window open during preview. Login or CAPTCHA pages may still require attention.
