Fill from Website

Paste an external job link into the Website column of your Notion table, one job per row. This tool reads the job website and fills available blank Company, Position, Location, Salary Range, Status (Applied), and Apply Date fields. Missing details stay blank; available details can still be filled. Rows with Company, Position, Status and Apply Date already filled are skipped, even when Location or Salary Range is blank. Your original Website link and existing values are kept.

Supports public job pages with readable job details and Greenhouse job boards. Location and Salary Range are optional Text properties. Pages requiring login or unsupported layouts are flagged for review. This workflow does not read Gmail.
